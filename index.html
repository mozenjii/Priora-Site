Workflow

What the product actually does.

01

Ingest the case

Staff upload clinical notes, labs, diagnosis codes, policy PDFs, denial letters, and treatment requests.

02

Extract and compare

Priora extracts the key facts, matches them to payer criteria, and flags missing evidence early.

03

Draft the response

The system outputs approval likelihood, a prior-auth summary, and a grounded appeal draft for review.
